## Quick Item Search 0.2.0

- Search gear by required level, slot, stats, rarity, binding type, and source. Try `rare lvl 25 2h wep boe`, `crafted gloves lvl 40 agil`, or `rings hp around 20`. The level window is configurable under AddOns options, and explicit ranges such as `lvl 20-27` use the bounds you type.
- Search class and racial spells by name or ID, including ranks your character hasn't learned. Try `player spell thorns`. Hover to preview a spell, press Enter to inspect it, or Shift-click to link it in chat.
- Ask gathering questions for common herbs and ores. Try `gather wintersb` or `what level can I gather thorium ore?`. Partial names match known resources, and equally good matches show their skill requirements.
- Scroll results one row at a time with the mouse wheel. Use the draggable scrollbar on longer lists.
- Press Enter to inspect the selected result and release the search box so you can open chat while the results stay visible.
